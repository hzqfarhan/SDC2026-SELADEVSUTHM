# SHIFTATLAS 3D - SELADEVS UTHM

We built a connected 3D career atlas for Malaysian learners: reviewed profiles, explainable role coverage, affordable learning, source-linked Malaysian labour context, SDG pathways and uncertain AI-adoption scenarios. Atlas Quest adds a playable route through the concepts.

Our 14-slide pitch uses editable Segoe UI text, low-poly visuals, the official SELADEVS team mark and original UN SDG artwork. The PDF matches the PowerPoint. The companion video includes the pitch, actual prototype views and our recorded E5 CUDA execution evidence. It runs approximately 6 minutes 51 seconds and includes captions.

- `SHIFTATLAS_PitchDeck.pptx`: editable presentation.
- `SHIFTATLAS_PitchDeck.pdf`: matching high-resolution handout.
- `SHIFTATLAS_PitchAndDemo.mp4`: pitch and product walkthrough.
- `SHIFTATLAS_Captions.srt`: companion captions.
- `PITCH_SCRIPT.md`: narration and source references.
- `DESIGN_SOURCES.md` and `ASSET_PROVENANCE.json`: original asset sources, briefs, rights and hashes.

We recorded one direct E5 CUDA request using fictional text. The six-role application gateway and four-pass Council have separate acceptance checks; the technical declaration records their current scope. Our prototype uses authored rubrics and hypothetical scenarios, with no claim of measured employment outcomes.

The video uses Windows synthetic narration and edited actual prototype captures. Codex substantially assisted the code, research and materials; OpenAI image generation produced the app icon and low-poly concept artwork. Model, data and third-party asset terms remain separate from the original project's Apache 2.0 licence. Official signed forms and private contact details are kept outside this public folder.
