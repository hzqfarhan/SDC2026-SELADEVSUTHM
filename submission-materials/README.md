# SHIFTATLAS 3D - SELADEVS UTHM

[Live application](https://sdc-2026-seladevsuthm.replit.app/) · [YouTube pitch and demo](https://youtu.be/e8jtdJy6deo) · [Source repository](https://github.com/hzqfarhan/SDC2026-SELADEVSUTHM)

[Complete submission index](SUBMISSION_INDEX.md). Our team has already handled the sdchack.my portal submission; these are our repository materials.

We built a connected 3D career atlas for Malaysian learners: reviewed profiles, explainable role coverage, affordable learning, source-linked Malaysian labour context, SDG pathways and uncertain AI-adoption scenarios. Atlas Quest adds a playable route through the concepts.

Career Council assigns one generative model four roles: profile reviewer, career explorer, learning planner and critic. Later stages build on earlier suggestions, and the critic challenges their reasoning to support comparison. E5 separately supplies semantic retrieval.

Our 14-slide pitch uses editable Segoe UI text, low-poly visuals, the official SELADEVS team mark and original UN SDG artwork. The PDF matches the PowerPoint. The companion video includes the pitch, actual prototype views and our recorded E5 CUDA execution evidence. The local export runs approximately 6 minutes 51 seconds and includes captions; the published YouTube player displays 6:50. The supplied SRT is available for a separate caption track.

Our prototype uses authored rubrics and hypothetical scenarios, with no claim of measured employment outcomes.
