## Main gap

The wider problem statement asks the system to track AI advancement and predict which jobs and skills will emerge or decline. SHIFTATLAS does not yet establish that predictive validity. A dated title-frequency experiment demonstrates a working input-to-recommendation change, rather than a reliable demand forecast. Present this as a preliminary proof of concept and a Phase 2 research priority. Do not relabel authored assumptions as measured trends merely to claim complete compliance.

The brief accepts a rough working proof of the single most important feature in the preliminary round (PDF pp7–8). Demonstrate Future Skills City, the baseline/adjusted comparison, its explicit toggle and Career Bridges contributions before spending time on visual extras.

## Rubric implications

- **Innovation and creativity, 30%:** connected spatial exploration and transparent source-to-score reasoning provide a concrete approach. Atlas Quest adds optional motivation. A learner pilot is needed before claiming engagement benefits.
- **Problem–solution fit, 25%:** the six required areas form a connected journey. Be candid about the unvalidated emerging/declining forecasts and US occupational reference transfer.
- **Feasibility, 25%:** 55 application tests, 18 offline worker tests and a production build pass, with desktop checks, refresh validation and local fallback. Actual judge access, Replit proof and a reproducible repository remain submission requirements.
- **Clarity, 20%:** the official proposal, declaration, deck and 5–7 minute combined video must agree. A coverage score is a rubric result, never an employment probability.

## Optional strengths and scope

The real DOSM state map, sourced institution directory/landmarks, inspectable 3D layers, 3D profile/resume, local privacy controls and Atlas Quest strengthen the experience. Learner progress and badges are optional under the brief (p6), not separately scored requirements. They do not replace reliable signals or matching. English-only content, incomplete mobile acceptance, no employer cohort view and no automatic background replanning are current limits.
