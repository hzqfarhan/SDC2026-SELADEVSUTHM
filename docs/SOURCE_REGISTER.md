## Learning and reference resources

- **Build your first data report**: [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/introduction-power-bi/). Free learning content; optional exams and some software services may cost extra. Suggested practice: 6 hours (author estimate).
- **Query a dataset with SQL**: [SQLBolt](https://sqlbolt.com/). Free browser exercises; no paid certificate assumed. Suggested practice: 8 hours (author estimate).
- **Python foundations**: [Python Software Foundation](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/). Free documentation. Prior programming concepts help; allow extra practice if new. Suggested practice: 16 hours (author estimate).
- **AI foundations & responsible use**: [IBM SkillsBuild](https://skillsbuild.org/adult-learners). Free catalogue; registration may be required. Confirm current course availability. Suggested practice: 8 hours (author estimate).
- **Communicate a technical finding**: [Google Technical Writing](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/one). Free course materials; focused on technical writing. Suggested practice: 6 hours (author estimate).
- **Evaluate data and model limits**: [Google ML Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course). Free lessons; Python and basic maths recommended. Suggested practice: 10 hours (author estimate).
- **Understand the sustainability goals**: [United Nations](https://sdgs.un.org/goals). Free reference material; self-directed study, not an accredited course. Suggested practice: 6 hours (author estimate).
- **Project delivery foundations**: [IBM SkillsBuild](https://skillsbuild.org/adult-learners). Free catalogue; registration may be required. Select project management content. Suggested practice: 10 hours (author estimate).
- **Design for accessibility**: [W3C WAI](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/). Free tutorials; some technical examples use HTML. Suggested practice: 8 hours (author estimate).
- **Digital security foundations**: [IBM SkillsBuild](https://skillsbuild.org/adult-learners). Free catalogue; registration may be required. Select cybersecurity content. Suggested practice: 10 hours (author estimate).
